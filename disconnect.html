Disconnected from QuickBooks

Claude Bookkeeping Agent MCP — GOSS Auto Parts Ltd.

The app's access to your QuickBooks Online company has been revoked. It can no longer read or change any QuickBooks data.

The saved sign-in on the company computer stops working right away. To remove it from that computer as well, delete the QUICKBOOKS_REFRESH_TOKEN and QUICKBOOKS_REALM_ID lines from the app's .env file.

To connect again later, run the app's sign-in command on the company computer and authorize it in QuickBooks.
