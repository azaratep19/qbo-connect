Privacy Policy

Claude Bookkeeping Agent MCP — GOSS Auto Parts Ltd. · Effective November 16, 2026

1. Who we are

Claude Bookkeeping Agent MCP (the "App") is a private, internal software tool operated by GOSS Auto Parts Ltd. ("we", "us"). The App is used only by our own staff to manage the QuickBooks Online books of GOSS Auto Parts Ltd. and its affiliated company Goss Capital (together, "our companies"). It is not offered to, sold to, or used by any other business or individual.

2. Information the App accesses

With authorization from a QuickBooks Online company admin, the App accesses our companies' QuickBooks Online accounting data through Intuit's Accounting API. This may include customers, vendors, employees (names and contact details), products and services, invoices, bills, payments, journal entries, accounts, tax codes and financial reports.

The App does not collect information from website visitors, does not use cookies or analytics, and does not access any QuickBooks company other than our own companies. Each company is connected separately, and its data is kept separate from the other's.

3. How the information is used

The information is used only to carry out bookkeeping requests made by our staff, such as looking up balances, recording transactions and producing reports. Requests are made in plain language through Anthropic's Claude AI assistant, which receives only the QuickBooks data needed to answer the request in that session. QuickBooks data is not used to train AI models.

4. Where the information is stored

The App runs on our own computer in Canada. QuickBooks sign-in tokens are stored locally on that computer and are never published or shared. The App does not keep its own copy or database of QuickBooks data; the records stay in QuickBooks Online. Error logs kept on the same computer contain technical details (such as Intuit request IDs and error messages) but no sign-in credentials.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share QuickBooks data with third parties, except (a) Intuit, as the provider of QuickBooks Online, (b) Anthropic, as the provider of the AI assistant, solely to process our own requests, and (c) where required by law.

6. Security

Access to QuickBooks uses Intuit's OAuth 2.0 authorization. Credentials are kept out of source code and stored only on our own computer. Access can be revoked at any time from QuickBooks Online, which immediately stops the App from reading or changing any data.

7. Retention and deletion

Disconnecting the App in QuickBooks Online revokes its access. Locally stored sign-in tokens and logs can be deleted from our computer at any time.

8. Your rights

Individuals whose personal information appears in our books may contact us to request access to or correction of that information, in accordance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial law.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
