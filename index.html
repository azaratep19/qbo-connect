Claude Bookkeeping Agent MCP

A private, internal tool of GOSS Auto Parts Ltd.

This app connects the QuickBooks Online companies of GOSS Auto Parts Ltd. and its affiliate Goss Capital to an AI bookkeeping assistant (Anthropic's Claude) through a self-hosted Model Context Protocol (MCP) server. It is used only by GOSS Auto Parts Ltd. staff for those companies' own bookkeeping. It is not offered, sold, or available to anyone else.

Connecting or reconnecting

The connection is started from the company's own computer by running the app's sign-in command, which opens the QuickBooks sign-in page. Only a QuickBooks Online company admin of the company being connected can authorize it.

Disconnecting

In QuickBooks Online, open Settings → Apps → My apps (or the Intuit "Connected apps" page), find Claude Bookkeeping Agent MCP, and choose Disconnect. This immediately revokes the app's access.
